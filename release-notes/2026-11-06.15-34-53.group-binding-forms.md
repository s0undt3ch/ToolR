**Breaking:** a `command_group(...)` call that is not a top-level `name = command_group(...)` assignment or a bare `command_group(...)` statement now fails the manifest build with "unsupported command declarations", and so does `CommandsTester`. Before, toolr dropped such a group and every command on it without a word and exited 0. That covers a tuple or chained target (`a, b = command_group(...), command_group(...)`), a walrus (`(g := command_group(...))`), and a call under an `if`, `try`, `with` or loop, or inside a `def` or class. Give each group its own top-level `name = command_group(...)` line, and move any platform check into the command body.
