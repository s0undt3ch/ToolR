**Breaking:** any `command_group(...)` call that isn't the whole value of a module-level assignment or bare statement now fails the manifest build with "unsupported command declarations", and so does `CommandsTester`. That covers a tuple or chained target (`a, b = command_group(...), command_group(...)`), a walrus (`(g := command_group(...))`), a call under an `if`, `try`, `with`, loop or `match`, a call inside a function, lambda, comprehension or class, and a call nested in another expression such as a decorator or call argument. Before, toolr dropped such a group and every command on it without a word and exited 0. Give each group its own top-level `name = command_group(...)` line, and move any platform check into the command body. A name-only lookup such as `command_group("ci")` can't create a group and is still fine anywhere, and a call that isn't toolr's (a local `def command_group`, a parameter, `self.command_group`) is never reported.
