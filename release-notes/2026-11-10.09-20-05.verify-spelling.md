**Breaking:** the installers and the action now share one spelling for how much to verify. Use `install.sh --verify=MODE` or `install.ps1 -Verify MODE`, where `MODE` is `full` (the default: SHA-256 and the SLSA attestation), `auto` (the attestation only when `gh` is available), `checksum` (SHA-256 only) or `none`. In the action use `verify: full` or `verify: none`. The old spellings still work for this release but are no longer documented: `--verify-attestation=require|auto|skip` and `-VerifyAttestation` become `full`, `auto` and `checksum`; `--no-verify` and `-NoVerify` still skip only the SHA-256; the action input `skip-attestation: true` becomes `verify: none` and now carries a deprecation message.
