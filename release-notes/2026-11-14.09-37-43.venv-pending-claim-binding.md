A failed first `uv sync` no longer lets toolr treat a later `tools/.venv` as its own. toolr now removes what the failed sync left, and only a finished sync's marker vouches for a venv: a sync that finds another toolr still building the venv waits for it instead of asking about it. A venv left by an interrupted sync, or files that appear in `tools/.venv` later, get the usual question before anything runs.
