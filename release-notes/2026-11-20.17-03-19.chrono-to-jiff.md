toolr.types.Time no longer passes a leap second (`:60`) through to Python, where it crashed the command. It is now turned into `:59`.

`toolr.types.DateTime` and `toolr.types.Time` now clamp a leap second (`:60`) to `:59`. `DateTime` also accepts `±HH` and `±HHMM` offsets, a comma as the decimal separator and omitted seconds, and rejects bracketed zone annotations, instants outside the supported range (such as `9999-12-31T23:59:59-01:00`) and more than 9 fractional-second digits. `Time` also rejects more than 9 fractional-second digits.
