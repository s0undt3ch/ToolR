`toolr.types.DateTime` and `toolr.types.Time` now turn a leap second (`:60`) into `:59` instead of passing it through to Python, where it crashed the command. Argument parsing changed in a few other ways:

- `DateTime` also accepts `±HH` and `±HHMM` offsets, a comma as the decimal separator and omitted seconds.
- `DateTime` rejects bracketed zone annotations and instants outside the supported range, such as `9999-12-31T23:59:59-01:00`.
- `DateTime` and `Time` reject more than 9 fractional-second digits.
